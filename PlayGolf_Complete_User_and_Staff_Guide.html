30-Second Daily Cheat Sheet for Front-Desk Reception Staff

Keep this page at the reception desk for quick daily reference
Customer Situation at Desk What the Staff Member Does What Happens Automatically
1. Member arrives for Driving Range (Walk-In) Click Scan QR Code on Staff Home and scan their phone (or type their PG-XXXXXX ID). Logs visit, awards +100 Loyalty Points, updates tier progress, and increments Visits Today.
2. Member arrives for a Pre-Booked Activity Scan their Booking QR Pass (PG-BK-XXXX) or click Check-In (+100 PTS) on Bookings Sheet. Confirms if PRE-PAID or COLLECT @ DESK, shows assigned Bay/Tee/Lane, and awards +100 pts.
3. Member claims a Free Coffee or Pro Shop Voucher Scan their Reward Voucher QR (or verify code under Rewards & Offers → Vouchers). Marks the voucher as Fulfilled/Used immediately so it cannot be used twice.
4. Member forgot their phone in the car Open Members tab, type their surname, and click +100 Check-In on their card. Credits their loyalty account immediately without needing their phone.
5. End of Day — Cashing Up the Reception Till Click the green End-of-Day App Takings banner on Staff Home → Click Save Z-Report (CSV). Shows total £ collected online vs. at desk, broken down by activity, with itemized receipts.
