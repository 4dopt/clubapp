## 1. The Core Commercial Reality: The "£14 Problem"

### 1.1 The Venue Owner's Dilemma
At most driving ranges and leisure golf facilities, the average transaction value is **precisely £14.00** — the cost of a single bucket of 100 range balls.

The current customer journey is a financial leak:
1. Golfer parks car.
2. Taps contactless card for **£14.00** at the range dispenser or reception counter.
3. Hits balls for 45–55 minutes in the bay.
4. Walks straight past the clubhouse bar, cafe, and pro shop directly to their car.
5. Drives home.

**The Financial Result**: The club collects £14.00 gross revenue. The food, drink, and retail facilities made **£0.00**.

### 1.2 The Two Ways PlayGolf Fixes This Immediately
1. **The Visit Multiplier**: Increasing the number of times existing members come to spend that £14.
2. **The Basket Lift**: Converting that £14-only customer into an £18 – £22 customer by cross-pollinating points into the bar and pro shop.

## 4. How the App Lifts the Basket from £14 to £21+ (The 50% Basket Growth Strategy)

### Step 1: Upgrading the Bucket Size (+£4.50)
- In the app, range packages are visible side-by-side:
  - 60 Balls: £11.50 (Earns 115 pts)
  - 100 Balls: £14.00 (Earns 140 pts)
  - 150 Balls: £18.50 (Earns 250 bonus pts — *Popular*)
- Because members want to level up to **Gold Tier** (which requires 1,000 pts), they deliberately choose the £18.50 basket instead of the £14 basket.
- **Immediate gain**: +£4.50 per visit (+32% basket growth).

### Step 2: The "Half-Price Drink" Cross-Sell (+£3.50 – £6.00)
- Right now, golfers don't enter the clubhouse because they have no reason to.
- The app offers a welcome or tier voucher: *"Redeem 300 points for a Half-Price Clubhouse Pint or Free Artisan Coffee with your Bay Booking"*.
- To redeem it, the golfer **must walk into the clubhouse**.
- Once inside, over **75% of golfers buy a sandwich, bacon roll, or a second pint**.
- The visit spend jumps from **£14.00 to £22.00 – £26.00**.

### Step 3: Monetizing Empty Weekday Bays (Zero Marginal Cost)
- Tuesday 10:30 AM: 12 bays sit empty. The lights are on, the lease is paid, but income is £0.
- A bay sitting empty has an inventory value that expires at 11:30 AM forever.
- Pushing a flash offer via the app filling just 6 idle weekday bays per day at £14 brings in **£84/day** = **£2,520/month** = **£30,240/year** of new money that previously evaporated.

## 5. Major Features & Visual Flow (Plain English for Board Members)

### Feature 1: The Digital VIP Card & Tier Psychology
- **What it is**: Replaces lost £3 plastic cards with a sleek, gold-accented mobile card.
- **The Psychology**: Shows Silver, Gold, or Platinum status and a progress bar (e.g., *"140 points to Gold VIP"*).
- **Why it stops churn**: A player with 850 points at your range will not go to a competitor's range to save £1 on balls; they want their VIP tier at your venue.

### Feature 2: 15-Second Bay & Activity Booking
- **What it is**: Members book TrackMan bays, 9-hole tee times, Putt Crazy adventure golf, AR darts, and PGA coaching from their phone.
- **The Financial Gain**: Over 42% of recreational sports bookings occur after 8:00 PM when clubhouse phone lines are closed. The app captures those £14 bookings while the reception desk is asleep.

### Feature 3: The Cross-Sell Marketplace
- **What it is**: Members redeem points for range balls, pro shop discounts, and bar drinks.
- **The Owner's Margin Protection**: It requires £100 in spending to earn a £3 coffee. That coffee costs the club **35 pence** in beans and milk. You spend 35p to generate £100 in customer loyalty.

### Feature 4: 2-Second Reception Staff Scanner
- **What it is**: Staff open the scanner on any existing counter tablet, PC, or mobile phone.
- **How it works**: Point camera at the customer's phone -> Beep -> Checked in -> Bay assigned.
- **Eliminates Queues**: Cuts check-in time from 2–3 minutes of manual paperwork down to 2 seconds.

### Feature 5: End-of-Day Payments & Till Report (The "Cashing Up" Screen)
- **What it is**: Under the "Manage the Club" section, management and reception staff can view the **Daily Payments & Till Report** at the tap of a button.
- **The End-of-Day Answers**:
  - Exactly how much the app has made today in total (£).
  - Breakdown of revenue by activity (TrackMan bays, 9-hole course, minigolf, darts, coaching).
  - Split between payments collected online in the app vs. pay at desk.
  - Itemized receipt journal of every transaction with member names, bay numbers, and time stamps.
- **Accountant-Ready Z-Report**: One-click download of the day's takings as a formatted CSV spreadsheet for immediate bookkeeping and till reconciliation.
