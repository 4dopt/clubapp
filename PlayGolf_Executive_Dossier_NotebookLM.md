## 1. The Core Commercial Reality: The "£10 Off-Peak Problem"

### 1.1 The Venue Owner's Dilemma
During off-peak timings, a golfer pays **£10.00 to hit 100 range balls**.

The current customer journey is a financial leak:
1. Golfer parks car.
2. Taps contactless card for **£10.00** (100 balls off-peak) at the range dispenser or reception counter.
3. Hits balls for 45–55 minutes in the bay.
4. Walks straight past the clubhouse bar, cafe, and pro shop directly to their car.
5. Drives home.

**The Financial Result**: The club collects £10.00 gross revenue. The food, drink, and retail facilities made **£0.00**.

### 1.2 The Two Ways PlayGolf Fixes This Immediately
1. **The Visit Multiplier**: Increasing the number of times existing members come to spend that £10+.
2. **The Basket Lift**: Converting that £10-only off-peak range customer into an £18 – £22 customer by cross-pollinating points into the bar, kitchen, and pro shop.

## 4. How the App Lifts the Basket from £10 to £18–£22+

### Step 1: Turning Off-Peak Range Visitors into Multi-Activity Players
- In the app, members see all club experiences side-by-side:
  - **Driving Range**: £10 for 100 balls (off-peak)
  - **9-Hole & 18-Hole Course**: From £12.50 (with £25 Electric Buggy Hire & £10 Club Hire add-ons)
  - **Putt Crazy Minigolf & Interactive AR Darts**
- Because members want to level up to **Gold Tier** (which requires 1,000 pts) and **Platinum Tier**, they play more frequently and bring friends or grandkids for Putt Crazy and Darts.

### Step 2: The "Coffee or Clubhouse Pint" Cross-Sell (+£6.00 – £12.00)
- Right now, £10 off-peak golfers don't enter the clubhouse because they have no reason to.
- The app offers a tier reward: *"Redeem 300 points for a Free Barista Coffee or Half-Price Clubhouse Pint"*.
- To redeem it, the golfer **must walk into the clubhouse**.
- Once inside, over **75% of golfers buy a pizza, burger, or a second drink**.
- The visit spend jumps from **£10.00 to £18.00 – £22.00+**.

### Step 3: Monetizing Empty Off-Peak Bays (Zero Marginal Cost)
- Tuesday 10:30 AM: 12 bays sit empty. The floodlights and TrackMan radars are on, the lease is paid, but income is £0.
- Pushing a daily offer via the app that fills just 6 idle off-peak bays per day at £10 brings in **£60/day** = **£1,800/month** = **£21,600/year** of new money that previously evaporated.

## 5. Major Features & Visual Flow (Plain English for Board Members)

### Feature 1: The Digital VIP Card & Tier Psychology
- **What it is**: Replaces lost £3 plastic cards with a sleek, gold-accented mobile card.
- **The Psychology**: Shows Silver, Gold, or Platinum status and a progress bar (e.g., *"140 points to Gold VIP"*).
- **Why it stops churn**: A player with 850 points at your range will not go to a competitor's range; they want their VIP tier at PlayGolf.

### Feature 2: 15-Second Activity & Partner Booking
- **What it is**: Members book TrackMan bays (via YourGolfBooking partner integration), 9-hole tee times, Putt Crazy adventure golf, and AR darts from their phone.
- **The Financial Gain**: Over 42% of recreational sports bookings occur after 8:00 PM when clubhouse phone lines are quiet.

### Feature 3: The Cross-Sell Marketplace
- **What it is**: Members redeem points for range balls, pro shop discounts, and bar drinks.
- **The Owner's Margin Protection**: It requires £100 in spending to earn a £3 coffee. That coffee costs the club **35 pence** in beans and milk. You spend 35p to generate £100 in customer loyalty.

### Feature 4: 2-Second Reception Staff Scanner
- **What it is**: Staff open the scanner on any existing counter tablet, PC, or mobile phone.
- **How it works**: Point camera at the customer's phone -> Beep -> Checked in -> Points awarded.
- **Eliminates Queues**: Cuts check-in time from 2–3 minutes down to 2 seconds.

### Feature 5: End-of-Day Payments & Till Report (The "Cashing Up" Screen)
- **What it is**: Under the "Manage the Club" section, management and reception staff can view the **Analytics & Till Report** at the tap of a button.
- **Accountant-Ready Z-Report**: One-click download of the day's takings as a formatted CSV spreadsheet for immediate bookkeeping and till reconciliation.
